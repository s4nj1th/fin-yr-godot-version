What is this study about?
We are studying a game that changes its enemy encounters to suit each player. You will play a short top-down dungeon game.

What will I do?
You will play for about 30 minutes, then answer a few questions on screen. If you take part in both sessions, the second session is on a different day. At the end of each session you will replay a few encounters you have already seen.

What is recorded?
Only what happens in the game (for example how long you took, how much damage you took, whether you reached the exit) and your answers to the questions. You are identified only by a code such as P07; your name is not stored with the game data.

Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, simply by telling the researcher; you can also ask for your data to be deleted, using your code, until the data are analysed.

Are there any risks?
The game contains no flashing lights or graphic content. If you feel uncomfortable or tired, stop at any time.
